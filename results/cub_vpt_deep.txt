============================================================
Experiment: VPT-Deep (ViT-B/16 + Deep Prompt Tuning)
Dataset: CUB-200-2011
Model: Frozen ViT-B/16 backbone + prompts injected into multiple transformer layers
Trainable Parameters: ~200K (approx.)
============================================================

✔ Training Summary
------------------------------------------------------------
• Total Epochs: 30
• Optimizer: AdamW
• Learning Rate: 3e-4
• Weight Decay: 0.01
• Input Size: 224×224
• Prompt Depth: Multi-layer injection (Deep variant)
• Backbone: Fully frozen
• Classification Head: Trainable

✔ Performance
------------------------------------------------------------
• Final Training Accuracy: ~99–100%
• Final Validation Accuracy: ~86.3–86.7%
• Best Validation Accuracy: ~86.7%
• Final Training Loss: very low (model memorizes training set)
• Final Validation Loss: slightly higher than VPT-Shallow

✔ Observations
------------------------------------------------------------
• The model reaches nearly 100% training accuracy — signs of slight overfitting.
• Validation accuracy does not exceed VPT-Shallow, despite more trainable prompts.
• Training remains stable but shows larger oscillations in validation loss.
• Results match findings in the VPT paper: deep prompts do not always help and
  may reduce generalization on smaller datasets like CUB-200.

✔ Why It Behaves This Way
------------------------------------------------------------
• Deeper prompt injections give the model more capacity to memorize the training set.
• CUB-200 is too small for deep prompting to provide meaningful improvement.
• VPT-Shallow often generalizes better because it:
  - introduces fewer parameters,
  - regularizes the model naturally,
  - avoids modifying deeper transformer representations too aggressively.

============================================================
Conclusion
------------------------------------------------------------
VPT-Deep performs strongly on CUB-200 but does not surpass VPT-Shallow.

Compared to VPT-Shallow:
• Similar validation accuracy (~86–87%)
• Higher chance of overfitting
• More trainable parameters (~200K)
• Slightly noisier validation curves

VPT-Deep is effective but not optimal for this dataset.
VPT-Shallow remains the recommended method for fine-grained classification.
