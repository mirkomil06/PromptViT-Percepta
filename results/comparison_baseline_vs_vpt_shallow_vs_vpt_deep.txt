============================================================
Comparison: Baseline ViT-B/16 vs. VPT-Shallow vs. VPT-Deep
Dataset: CUB-200-2011
Backbone: ViT-B/16
============================================================

### ✔ Model Summary
------------------------------------------------------------
1. Baseline ViT-B/16
   - Full fine-tuning (all 86M parameters trainable)
   - Highest computational cost
   - Prone to heavy overfitting

2. VPT-Shallow
   - Frozen ViT backbone
   - ~10K trainable prompt parameters
   - Prompts injected only at the input layer

3. VPT-Deep
   - Frozen ViT backbone
   - ~200K trainable parameters (more prompts across multiple layers)
   - Higher capacity than shallow prompting

============================================================
### ✔ Accuracy Comparison (Validation Set)
------------------------------------------------------------
| Model            | Train Acc | Val Acc | Trainable Params |
|------------------|-----------|----------|-------------------|
| Baseline ViT-B/16 | ~98–99%   | **36–37%** | ~86,000,000       |
| VPT-Shallow       | ~96–97%   | **86–87%** | ~10,000           |
| VPT-Deep          | ~99–100%  | **86.3–86.7%** | ~200,000        |

============================================================
### ✔ Key Insights
------------------------------------------------------------
• **VPT-Shallow provides the best generalization**:
  - Highest stability
  - Best validation accuracy
  - Lowest overfitting
  - ~1000× fewer parameters than full fine-tuning

• **Baseline ViT-B/16 fails to generalize**:
  - Nearly perfect training accuracy (99%)
  - Extremely poor validation accuracy (36–37%)
  - Strong overfitting due to small dataset

• **VPT-Deep does not outperform VPT-Shallow**:
  - Reaches 100% training accuracy → slight overfitting
  - Validation accuracy nearly identical to shallow
  - Additional prompt layers offer no improvement on CUB-200

============================================================
### ✔ Overfitting Behavior
------------------------------------------------------------
• Baseline: Severe overfitting after a few epochs  
• VPT-Shallow: Minimal overfitting, smooth training  
• VPT-Deep: Slight overfitting (train 100%, val fluctuates slightly)

============================================================
### ✔ Parameter Efficiency
------------------------------------------------------------
| Model        | Trainable Params | Reduction vs. Baseline |
|--------------|------------------|--------------------------|
| Baseline     | 86,000,000       | —                        |
| VPT-Shallow  | 10,000           | **~8600× fewer**         |
| VPT-Deep     | 200,000          | **~430× fewer**          |

VPT methods drastically reduce the number of trainable parameters.

============================================================
### ✔ Final Conclusion
------------------------------------------------------------
**VPT-Shallow is the optimal method for this project.**

It provides:
• The highest validation accuracy  
• The best generalization  
• The smallest number of trainable parameters  
• The most stable training curves  

VPT-Deep offers more capacity but **does not improve performance** on CUB-200.

The Baseline ViT-B/16 demonstrates that **full fine-tuning is not suitable** for small fine-grained datasets.
