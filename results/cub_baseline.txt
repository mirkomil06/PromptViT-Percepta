============================================================
Experiment: Baseline ViT-B/16 (Full Fine-Tuning)
Dataset: CUB-200-2011
Model: ViT-B/16 (trainable backbone + trainable head)
Trainable Parameters: ~86M
============================================================

✔ Training Summary
------------------------------------------------------------
• Total Epochs: 60
• Optimizer: AdamW
• Learning Rate: 3e-4
• Weight Decay: 0.01
• Input Size: 224×224
• Backbone: Fully trainable (no freezing)
• Augmentations: Standard (resize, crop, flip)

✔ Performance
------------------------------------------------------------
• Final Training Accuracy: ~98–99%
• Final Validation Accuracy: ~36–37%
• Best Validation Accuracy: ~36.7%
• Final Training Loss: ~1.0
• Final Validation Loss: ~3.3–3.4

✔ Observations
------------------------------------------------------------
• Model heavily overfits:
  - Training accuracy approaches 100%
  - Validation accuracy plateaus early
• ViT-B/16 requires very large datasets to fine-tune effectively.
• Full fine-tuning is unstable on small fine-grained datasets like CUB-200.
• Despite long training, generalization remains poor.

✔ Why Performance Is Low
------------------------------------------------------------
• Too many trainable parameters (~86M) for a small dataset.
• Fine-grained bird classification requires subtle discrimination:
  - colors, beaks, wings, patterns, textures
• CUB-200 is not large enough to support full ViT fine-tuning.
• The model learns training images but fails to generalize.

============================================================
Conclusion
------------------------------------------------------------
The baseline ViT-B/16 model demonstrates strong overfitting and
poor generalization on CUB-200.

Compared to VPT methods:
• Much lower validation accuracy (~36% vs. ~87%)
• ~1000× more trainable parameters
• Lower stability and higher training cost

This experiment serves as a reference point showing that
full fine-tuning is not suitable for small fine-grained datasets.
